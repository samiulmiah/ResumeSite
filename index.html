about

To me, an engineer is a problem solver, and often the most impactful problems occur in our own biology.

Throughout my experience in injury biomechanics research, creating custom test fixtures, CAD, experimental analysis and programming not only have I become acustomed to finding answers to better our understanding of the human body and its properties. But also explaining these unfamiliar topics to those who may one day benifit from them.

Although explanations aren't always the most intuitive.

Which is why through a multidisciplinary approach I hope to continue creating models that bridge the gap between engineers and patients.
